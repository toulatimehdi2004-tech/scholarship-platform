FOREIGNER PHYSICAL EXAMINATION FORM (外国人体格检查表)
OFFICIAL MEDICAL PROTOCOL & HOSPITAL GUIDE FOR MOROCCAN APPLICANTS 🇲🇦
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS THIS FORM?
The Foreigner Physical Examination Form is a mandatory document required by the China Scholarship Council (CSC), 
Chinese universities, and the Chinese Embassy in Rabat / Visa Application Center for the X1/X2 student visa.

2. VALIDITY PERIOD:
- Strictly valid for 6 MONTHS from the date of completion.
- Recommendation: Perform the medical examination between December and March for September intake.

3. WHERE TO COMPLETE IT IN MOROCCO:
- Must be completed at an official public Moroccan hospital (Centre Hospitalier Universitaire - CHU Ibn Sina Rabat, CHU Ibn Rochd Casablanca, Hôpital Militaire, or provincial public hospitals) or certified polyclinic.
- Must have:
  a) A recent passport photograph glued to the top-right corner.
  b) The official ROUND STAMP (Cachet Rond de l'Hôpital) stamped OVER the photo so half of the stamp is on the photo and half on the form.
  c) The attending physician's signature and stamp at the bottom.
  d) The hospital's official seal and final conclusion: "Suggested: Fits for study abroad / Normal".

4. MANDATORY ATTACHED LABORATORY & CLINICAL REPORTS (MUST BE ATTACHED):
Chinese authorities require the laboratory slips attached to the form:
  1. Chest X-Ray Report (Radiographie Pulmonaire / Telethorax) - Testing for Tuberculosis.
  2. Electrocardiogram (ECG / Électrocardiogramme).
  3. Blood Test Serology (Sérologie Sanguine):
     - HIV / SIDA (Negative)
     - Syphilis / RPR / TPHA (Negative)
     - Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg)
  4. Blood Routine Test (NFS / Hémogramme complet).

5. CRITICAL CHECKLIST BEFORE SUBMISSION:
[ ] Every single line filled (no empty boxes; write "Normal" or "None").
[ ] Official round hospital stamp on doctor's signature.
[ ] Official round stamp overlapping passport photo.
[ ] Attached laboratory printouts (X-ray, ECG, Blood test).
[ ] High-resolution color scan in PDF format (under 5 MB).
