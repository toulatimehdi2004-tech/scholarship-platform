# Scholarship / Study-Abroad Platforms — Feature Research (China-focused lens)

Context: China-focused scholarship + university finder (Next.js + Django), AI chat, virtual tours, document checklist, deadline countdown, EN/FR/AR, freemium. Target: Africa/Middle East students applying to Chinese universities.
Method: web search + page fetch, Oct 2026. Top picks only, no fluff.

---

## 1. Scholars4Dev — https://www.scholars4dev.com/
- **Standardized scholarship cards:** every listing shows Deadline / Study-in / Course-starts / Degree-level / Last-updated in the same slots — scannable in seconds.
- **Curated "Top N" lists:** Top 25 gov scholarships, Fully-Funded-only list, per-country compilations (USA/UK/Canada/Australia/Europe tabs) — SEO + decision shortcut.
- **Developing-country framing + Tips library:** filters narrative to Global South; 8 short guides (e.g. "Don't apply to every scholarship", "Am I qualified?") reduce unqualified applications.

## 2. ScholarshipPortal / Studyportals — https://www.scholarshipportal.com/ (studyportals.com)
- **AI Student Advisor "Sophia":** chat finds programmes AND scholarships, compares admission requirements side-by-side; understands budget ("medicine under €20k/yr"), background, English level; works in EN/ES/FR/DE/ZH.
- **200k+ programme comparison engine:** Bachelors/Masters/PhD/Distance portals with identical filter UX (discipline, location, tuition, duration) — copy for China programme catalogue.
- **Nationality + currency personalization:** asks nationality/currency once, then localizes fees, visa and scholarship relevance everywhere.
- **Own-scholarship flywheel:** €10,000 Studyportals Scholarship drives accounts + email opt-ins; 4-step apply (stay updated → confirm acceptance → submit).

## 3. CampusChina / CSC official — https://www.campuschina.org/ (apply: https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn)
- **Official CSC online system:** Program Category Type A/B + Agency Number matching (auto-shows agency name), withdraw-and-resubmit before processing, print-application-form step — authoritative flow to mirror.
- **University/major catalogue by discipline:** "Catalog of Discipline Field → Major" download + filter by teaching language (Chinese/English); pre-admission letter upload slot.
- **Hard gates made explicit:** HSK 3/4 by level + CSCA test (mandatory for undergrads, fixed exam dates) + Foreigner Physical Exam form — checklist with qualifying thresholds, not vague "requirements".
- **Embassy-track timeline:** research → contact university → online submit by date → embassy review → check result in-system (July/Aug); status checked by applicant in portal.

## 4. DAAD — https://www.daad.de/en/ (+ scholarship database)
- **Scholarship database with granular filters:** filter by applicant status (undergrad/master/PhD), field, destination, duration, language — exact pattern for CSC/provincial/uni-scholarship filtering.
- **"My DAAD" + advisory network:** saved list/account + 60+ regional offices and info centres for in-person advice — model for FR/AR regional counselor coverage.
- **Country + funding guides:** per-country pages combine money + visa + cost-of-living + checklists in one place (vs. scattering across blog).

## 5. Chevening — https://www.chevening.org/
- **Eligibility checker + "Are you ready?" quiz:** instant pass/fail, 5 attempts, questions on citizenship/work-hours/leadership/career plan — best anti-spam pattern for CSC eligibility.
- **Work-experience calculator:** enter each job (weeks × hours) → auto-sums toward 2,800-hour requirement with live total — copy as HSK-hours / GPA / age / CSCA-readiness calculator.
- **Course finder + 3-choice rule:** must pick 3 different UK master's courses, explain fit per course; structured essays (leadership, networking, career plan) + references + interview pipeline with public timeline.
- **Guidance hub:** "common application errors", essay scoring criteria, FAQs — reduces support load.

## 6. ApplyBoard — https://www.applyboard.com/
- **AI-guided search over 150k programs / 1,500 institutions:** profile → recommendations; "apply to multiple programs at once" with built-in quality checks (claims 95% success).
- **360 Solutions:** upsell rail beyond application — language-test vouchers, student loans, accommodation, visa support — direct freemium→paid template.
- **Trust engine:** live counters (1.5M students, 180+ nationalities), per-destination institution cards, video testimonials — high conversion for first-time abroad students.
- **Three-sided platform:** separate funnels for Students / Recruitment Partners / Institutions — relevant if onboarding Chinese universities + African agents.

## 7. Yocket — https://yocket.com/
- **Admits & Rejects + acceptance-rate compare:** crowdsourced profiles (CGPA, admits) with "General vs Yocket acceptance rate" bars per university (e.g. CMU 11% vs 32%) — most copy-worthy social proof.
- **College Finder + calculators:** CGPA→GPA, SGPA→%, GRE→GMAT converters; per-country hubs (Cost / Exams / Visa / Jobs / Intakes) — high-retention utility content.
- **Premium 1:1 counselling + Loan Finder:** profile building, university shortlist, SOP/LOR editing, visa prep; 30+ lender comparison — monetization blueprint.
- **Community + conversion:** Feed Q&A, Events, "Connect 1:1 with expert" booking, app download push.

## 8. WeMakeScholars — https://www.wemakescholars.com/
- **Lender comparison table:** 20+ banks/NBFCs side-by-side (ROI from 8.9%, collateral, max amount, "you save X%") + negotiated-rate badge — adapt to scholarship stipend/coverage comparison.
- **2-minute eligibility checker + Dedicated Financial Officer:** quick form → personal officer, "callback in 45 mins", end-to-end to disbursal — model for "CSC advisor in 24h".
- **26,000+ scholarship DB paired with loans:** content + financing in one funnel; cost-of-living and WhatsApp-group content per destination.
- **Referral loop:** "Refer & Earn up to ₹50k / ₹3k per disbursal", share via WhatsApp — cheap acquisition for Africa/MENA.

## 9. Study-in-China admission portals (CampusInChina / CUCAS pattern) — https://campusinchina.com/ (+ https://cucas.cn/)
- **4-step apply flow with time promises:** Discover (10k+ programs, 700 unis; filter subject/city/tuition/language/scholarship) → Apply (~20 min, $0 fee) → Decision (2–4 wks) → Arrival (pickup/housing/visa/registration) — lowest-friction China-specific funnel.
- **Scholarship-type cards with odds:** CSC / Provincial / University / Confucius cards + published success rates (UG 94%, MBBS 89%, Gov-scholarship 76%) — transparency that builds trust.
- **Free application + advisor chat:** "Start free, advisor from day one" CTA; FAQ kills top objections (no Chinese needed, English-taught + 1-yr preparatory Chinese).
- **City-first browsing:** Cities as entry point (cost, climate, universities) — important for students who choose Beijing/Shanghai vs. low-cost provinces.

## 10. Campus France — https://www.campusfrance.org/en
- **Nationality-aware funding wizard:** "My nationality → eligible scholarships" + "What program are you looking for?" (Undergrad/Postgrad/PhD/English-taught/Art/Distance) — best pattern for EN/FR/AR audience.
- **Step-by-step arrival checklist:** visa → CVEC fee → housing → budget planner → bank → health insurance → French learning → sponsor/buddy — directly reusable as China pre-departure checklist.
- **100+ local offices map + France Alumni + e-ambassador blog:** offline trust + alumni network + student stories by region (incl. Morocco/Kuwait/Senegal offices) — blueprint for Africa/MENA city offices.
- **Quality signals:** "Bienvenue en France" label (189 certified institutions), tuition-fee transparency, rankings hub.

## 11. Buddy4Study (app + web) — https://www.buddy4study.com/ (1M+ Play installs, 4.4★)
- **Profile-based matching + deep filters:** class/state/type (Govt/International/Girls/SC-ST/Minority/Disabled) over 10,000 scholarships — model for (degree/country/GPA/budget/language) matching.
- **Follow + alerts:** "Follow" a scholarship → push/SMS/email on deadline extension + result declaration; monthly bulletin + newsletter — retention mechanic.
- **Scholar Tracking System + doc upload:** in-app application status, document upload, verification updates — lightweight tracker to copy before full CRM.
- **EMI calculator + psychometric/stream tests:** adjacent utilities that keep pre-decision users in-app.

## 12. ScholarshipOwl — https://scholarshipowl.com/ (web + iOS/Android app)
- **One profile → one-click multi-apply + essay reuse:** single essay auto-adapted to multiple applications — biggest time-saver; copy for CSC + university + provincial triple-apply.
- **Match quality UX:** algorithm filters to "only where you're competitive" (GPA/major/background); sort/filter/favorite/hide; New/Seen bolding + orange badge counts — ends "messy search" fatigue.
- **Auto-reapply + real-time status tracker:** automatically re-enters recurring scholarships; Submitted-tab tracker + deadline dual timer (quick-glance + detailed) — pair with our deadline countdown.
- **Verified-only DB + swipe mobile UI:** all scholarships pre-screened; swipe-right-to-save app pattern — trust + speed on low-bandwidth phones.

## TOP 10 feature ideas most worth copying (ranked by student value)

1. **Eligibility quiz + readiness calculator (Chevening-style, extended to CSC: age/degree/HSK/CSCA/GPA)** — kills wasted applications, the #1 frustration for Africa/MENA applicants.
2. **One-profile, one-click multi-apply + essay reuse (ScholarshipOwl)** — apply once to CSC + provincial + university awards; 10x less form pain.
3. **Coverage comparison table (WeMakeScholars-style: tuition/stipend/housing/insurance side-by-side)** — students choose funding, not just universities.
4. **Application tracker + deadline dual-timer + push/SMS/WhatsApp alerts (ScholarshipOwl/Buddy4Study)** — never miss CSC/embassy windows; pairs with existing countdown.
5. **Multilingual AI advisor that knows budget + language + background (Studyportals Sophia)** — extend current AI chat to compare requirements and costs in EN/FR/AR.
6. **Admits & Rejects + acceptance-odds bars (Yocket)** — crowdsourced proof ("Moroccan bachelor → CSC at X") beats marketing copy.
7. **Cost / visa / exam hubs + converters per country→China corridor (Yocket/Campus France)** — living-cost planner, HSK/CSCA explainer, visa checklist per origin country.
8. **Saved lists + Follow-for-result alerts (DAAD/Buddy4Study)** — shortlist universities/scholarships, get deadline-extension and result pings.
9. **Fast human callback: advisor/counselor booking + WhatsApp (WeMakeScholars/Yocket)** — "advisor in 24h" converts freemium where chat alone fails.
10. **Pre-departure checklist + alumni stories + community Q&A (Campus France/ApplyBoard)** — pickup, housing, bank, insurance, city guides + success videos close the last-mile anxiety.
